# My understanding of DevOps
DevOps is a culture that brings teams together to build, test, and deploy high-quality software faster.
It breaks down silos, enabling developers, operations managers, QAs, and testers to communicate effectively.
Through continuous integration and continuous delivery powered by pipelines and automation, DevOps delivers reliable, secure, and high-performing software.

- devops is a culture mindset to collaborate with development team and operation team by reducing time to market , automating the repetetive task ,and scale  to the end user.
  # DevOps vs. Agile
#### A common interview question is distinguishing DevOps from Agile. While both aim for efficiency, they focus on different stages:

- Agile is a project management methodology focused on iterative development.  It breaks projects into small increments (sprints) to gather customer feedback and adapt requirements quickly.     It primarily addresses the development phase. 
- DevOps focuses on the entire software lifecycle, including deployment and maintenance.  It emphasizes the collaboration between development and operations and relies heavily on automation     tools (like CI/CD pipelines) to deliver the code Agile produces.
